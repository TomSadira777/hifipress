The Joint of No Return

Far Out Chronicles: Book 1

"Star Trek meets Cheech & Chong!"

Charlie Hong is on the run from both sides of the law.

He's spent the last year in hiding, cultivating one last hare-brained scheme to escape his troubles: a clandestine marijuana operation. Just as he's about to cash in, his cash crop is stolen by a bright light appearing in the night sky. After catching a ride with one of his plants, he finds himself thrown into a world far stranger than anything he could have possibly imagined.

Does the stoned stowaway have what it takes to free the Starseed, a massive living spaceship overflowing with bizarre lifeforms, from the clutches of a mysterious force field trapping it in Earth's orbit?

Is he up for the challenge of fighting an evil Reptilian race bent on enslaving humanity?

More importantly, before he decides anything: does he have time to light one up?

Sci-fi

Red Awakening

A Martian Manifesto

People of Earth, your presence here is neither requested nor welcome.

We mean you no harm. That is, we mean you no harm as long as you stay away from the planet you once called Mars, which you will now refer to by its rightful, ancient name: Sango Lumos.

When we first awoke we meant you no harm; we were simply astonished to be alive. And yet you sent your killing machine after us. You took my beloved from me.

Therefore, any spacecraft or vehicles, manned or unmanned, that land on Sango Lumos will be claimed as our property.

Any Earth passengers are free to remain on Sango Lumos until their life support systems run out.

Any passengers who become hostile will be hurled into space in the general direction of Earth.

Hear this and remember: we who have awakened declare that from this sol forward, this red planet, fourth from our shared local star, is ours once again. Sango Lumos lives!

Come at your own risk—but. please, if you have any sense of self-preservation, just don’t come at all.
